About the designer


We’re a young couple who believe clothing carries a story. What started as a shared love for creativity has grown into a collection of handmade knitwear and dresses, each piece created with patience, care, and passion.

Living in the Swiss Alps, we draw inspiration from the mountains’ purity, strength, and timeless beauty. Some designs take over a month to complete, with every stitch placed by hand, making each garment truly one of a kind.

We specialize in creating clothing for driven, successful individuals — entrepreneurs, hotel owners, and those who understand the value of time, quality, and artistry. Our pieces are designed for people who move fast in their work but still appreciate the slow, deliberate craft of something made to last.

For us, clothing is more than fashion. It is craftsmanship, patience, and intimacy woven into form — a way of creating something lasting in a world that often moves too fast.

🧶 Welcome to our story.

Frequently asked questions


What makes Shall We knitwear special?

Each Shall We piece is hand-crafted in the Swiss Alps with precision, patience, and poetry. Every stitch carries emotion — a union of artistry, heritage, and modern elegance. Our knitwear is not mass-produced; it’s created slowly, intentionally, and designed to last for generations.

Are all pieces handmade?

Yes. Every Shall We item is individually hand-knitted by the designer using the finest yarns. No two garments are ever completely identical — that’s the beauty of craftsmanship.

What kind of yarns do you use?

We work with premium natural fibers such as merino wool, cashmere, alpaca, and mohair, carefully sourced for softness, durability, and ethical standards. Each yarn is chosen to complement the garment’s design and emotional tone.

How can I order a custom piece?

Every customer is personally attended to by the designer. You can contact us on Instagram or WhatsApp to discuss your design ideas, colors, yarn, size, and all the details. Each piece is made to your individual preferences, ensuring a truly unique creation.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide from Switzerland. Every garment is carefully packaged with a personal note and authenticity certificate. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location.

How long does delivery take?

Since every Shall We item is made to order, the creation process may take 3–6 weeks. Once shipped, delivery usually takes:
Switzerland: 1–3 business days
Europe: 3–7 business days
Worldwide: 7–14 business days
You’ll receive a tracking number once your piece is on its way.

How should I care for my knitwear?

Each piece comes with specific care instructions, but in general:
Hand wash gently in cold water with mild wool detergent.
Dry flat on a towel, away from direct sunlight.
Do not hang (it may stretch the fibers).
Store folded, ideally in a breathable cotton bag.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Because every garment is handmade and custom-sized, we unfortunately cannot accept returns or exchanges unless the item arrives damaged or defective. However, we’re always here to help — contact us within 3 days of delivery and we’ll make it right.

How can I stay informed about new creations?

Follow us on Instagram @shallwe.fashion and save our WhatsApp contact to discuss your next unique piece. This is the best way to get personal attention and be among the first to see new designs.
